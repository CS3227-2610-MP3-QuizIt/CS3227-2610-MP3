A SIMPLE PATH TO BETTER PRACTICE

Prepare. Practice. Understand.

AI supports the work; people stay in control. Grades are calculated by the backend, and AI observations deserve a thoughtful review.

  1. 01

    Prepare with care

    A teacher generates and reviews the quiz, then publishes a fixed set of questions for the class.

  2. 02

    Practice with guidance

    Students answer at their own pace. AI hints are privately checked for relevance and answer leakage before being shown.

  3. 03

    Reflect on the results

    Students see explanations after submission. Administrators can request class insights when everyone finishes.
